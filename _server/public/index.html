HOW TO RACE

NATIVE / IN-BROWSER

The fastest path: the game runs locally in this tab through EmulatorJS. No host instance or video stream required.

PLAY IN BROWSER

1. TAKE A SEAT (STREAM FALLBACK)

Click TAKE P1 or TAKE P2 to drive the live LAN cabinet. Both seats are needed for a versus race; one seat works for solo/time attack.

2. PRESS START

Use PRESS START to wake the cabinet, then navigate the game's menus with your D-pad or left stick. Cross/A confirms, Circle/B backs out.

3. RACE

Choose VS BATTLE for 2-player split-screen. The host cabinet runs the game; your inputs travel over the LAN. For a host-free race, use PLAY IN BROWSER.
